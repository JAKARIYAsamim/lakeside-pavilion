Purbachal, in numbers.

The new town rising on the eastern edge of Dhaka, and the ground Lakeside Pavilion stands on.

  • 6,200+acres

    The largest planned township in Bangladesh, about 25 square kilometres.

  • 30sectors

    Laid out sector by sector under one master plan by RAJUK.

  • 25,000+plots

    Residential plots planned across the township.

  • 12.5km

    Of expressway, the “300 Feet” road, linking Purbachal to Kuril in Dhaka.

  • 11km

    Of elevated metro to Purbachal, under construction as part of MRT Line 1.

  • 8%

    Of the township is planned as lakes and canals.

Figures describe Purbachal New Town as a whole, from RAJUK and published reports. They are approximate and are not figures for this project.
