## The area facts section

Under the About section, on a dark background: six big numbers about Purbachal
New Town that count up once when the section scrolls into view. The "Location"
menu link scrolls to it. To edit, find `AREA FACTS` in `index.html`; each fact
is one `<li>` with the number, its unit and one line. Styles are in the
`AREA FACTS` part of `css/styles.css`.

The figures are about the township, not this project, and were checked on
11 November 2026. Sources disagree slightly, so the page uses rounded values:

| Fact on the page | What the sources say |
| --- | --- |
| 6,200+ acres, largest planned township | 6,213 acres (RAJUK project page), 6,227 acres / about 25 sq km (Wikipedia), 6,150 acres (PPP Office) |
| 30 sectors | Wikipedia, PPP Office |
| 25,000+ plots | 25,016 residential plots planned (PPP Office), 26,000 allocated (Prothom Alo) |
| 12.5 km expressway | Kuril flyover to Kanchan Bridge, the "300 Feet" road (Wikipedia, Bangladesh Post) |
| 11 km metro, under construction | 11.37 km elevated Purbachal branch of MRT Line 1 (The Daily Star, The Business Standard). No opening date is shown because the target has moved. |
| 8% lakes and canals | Wikipedia only. This one has a single source: confirm it or remove it. |
